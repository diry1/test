How points work

+1 for each distinct model, +20 for the first discovery of each brand, and +1 for each distinct identified generation. Engine and trim choices, photos, and repeat sightings add no extra points.

Example: your first BMW X5 earns 21 points; identifying it as an F15 adds 1 more. Identifying a different X5 generation adds 1 point. Scores reflect your saved sightings, including earlier sightings. Deleting the last record of a discovery removes its points.
